3. **Failed sub-hypothesis (tFqMutations M1).** The derivation of the CG-aft Level change assumed that the 6.2 n/alpha (with the elevator's own lift) is below the alpha-only 14.90 g/rad. 4. **n/alpha definition.** 6.2 (p.77) is followed literally (steady state for an incremental pitch-control deflection at constant speed, i.e. (V/g)/T_theta2, elevator lift included). It differs from the M5-A lin.n_alpha (alpha only) by about 1 % at CG 25 % and 3 % at CG 30 %. Some texts use the alpha-only value; a reviewer may prefer it. Both are available.
5. **Speed divergence classification.** At low speed, high altitude and CG 30 % the bare F-16 splits its short period into real roots, one unstable (e.g. +0.159 1/s at 25,000 ft, Mach 0.35, CG 30 %), which the M5-A classifier names `short_period` NOT_OSCILLATORY although its participation is u/theta dominated (0.68). T2_speed_divergence_s counts it (participation criterion), so 3.2.1.1 is "worse than Level 3" there (T2 = 4.3 s < 6 s). This is the expected relaxed-static-stability behaviour of the bare airframe, but the interpretation (pitch-speed divergence counted as speed divergence) deserves review.
6. **Roll mode not found at low speed.** At 20 default-grid points the lateral real root with the largest |lambda| fails the roll signature (phi has the largest participation), so tau_R_s is MODE_MISSING and those points are excluded (counted) for 3.3.1.2; 3.3.1.4 marks them MODE_UNCLASSIFIED. The classifier thresholds are M5-A design choices.
7. **Table VI Level 3 increment.** The .005(omega_nd^2|phi/beta|_d - 20) Level 3 increment has no base zeta_d*omega_nd ("-" in Table VI); it is not encoded (coverage NO-REQUIREMENT). A conservative reading would require zeta_d*omega_nd >= 0 + increment at Level 3.
